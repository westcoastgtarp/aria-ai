HIPAA-ORIENTED DESIGN

Privacy & Security

Architecture targets strong healthcare privacy controls, but compliance requires contracts, policies, risk analysis, staff training, and operational controls in addition to code.

🔐

Encryption

Production design requires encryption in transit and at rest with managed keys.

👤

Role-based access

Support, billing, engineering, Lifeline, and privacy roles are separated.

🧾

Audit controls

PHI access and emergency disclosures require attributable, tamper-resistant logs.

🚨

Break-glass access

Emergency elevation is time-limited, justified, logged, and reviewable.

🤝

Vendor controls

Production vendors touching ePHI require appropriate due diligence and BAAs where applicable.

🧠

AI separation

Original transcripts remain distinct from AI summaries and safety classifications.
