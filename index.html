LIFELINE RESPONSE FLOW

How the 2-minute contact window works

When the active Aria conversation reaches the configured serious distress threshold, the member is given two minutes to press Call Emergency Contact. If the member does not press it before the timer expires, the prototype prepares an alert for the approved emergency contact and includes location only when permission was previously granted.

The emergency contact is then presented with calmer choices first: Send Check-In, View Location, and Call Member. No real message, call, or location sharing occurs in this prototype.
