SYSTEM POLICIES

System Policies

System Policies is the internal reference area for approved company rules and operating standards that govern how Aria AI staff use company systems, access information, perform work, and handle member or employee data.

This area is intended to organize policies covering acceptable system use, account and access security, privacy and data handling, employee conduct and disciplinary procedures, billing and refund handling, incident and escalation response, change management, backups and recovery, device and infrastructure standards, and other department-specific operating requirements.

Policies should be version-controlled, assigned an owner, reviewed on a defined schedule, and acknowledged by employees when required. Changes that affect staff access, privacy, security, billing, HR processes, or member-facing services should be reviewed by the appropriate department before they become active company policy.

Prototype status: this section describes the intended policy framework. Final production policies require formal company approval and, where applicable, legal, privacy, security, HR, or compliance review.
