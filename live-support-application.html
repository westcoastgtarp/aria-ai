Live Support Specialist

This is a specialized, non-clinical, chat-only support position supporting Aria members when the Lifeline system identifies sustained or serious distress signals.

You will only be accepted into this position if you pass your Support Specialist Certification Test.
Submitting an application or completing an interview does not grant access to the Member Communication queue.

Minimum and preferred qualifications

  • At least 1 year of customer support experience.
  • Experience working in de-escalation environments or handling distressed, angry, frightened, or overwhelmed customers.
  • Professional emotional-support experience or closely related experience is strongly preferred.
  • Strong written communication skills for live, chat-only member support.
  • Ability to remain calm, empathetic, professional, and nonjudgmental during high-emotion conversations.
  • Ability to recognize when a conversation exceeds the role and follow Aria escalation procedures instead of improvising medical, mental-health, or emergency advice.
  • Training or experience in crisis communication, suicide/self-harm awareness, abuse/domestic-violence awareness, Mental Health First Aid, or similar de-escalation programs is preferred.
  • Ability to work scheduled 24/7 coverage, including nights, weekends, and holidays when assigned.
  • Ability to protect sensitive member information and follow Aria privacy, access, and confidentiality requirements.
  • Successful completion of Aria's Support Specialist Certification Test and supervised readiness review before independent queue access.
Role boundary: Live Support Specialists are not therapists, clinicians, emergency dispatchers, or medical advisers. The role provides calm human support, keeps the member engaged in chat, follows approved protocols, recognizes escalation cues, and recommends appropriate resources when required. The position does not diagnose, prescribe, change medication instructions, or promise emergency intervention.
Please do not submit Social Security numbers, banking details, government ID numbers, medical information, or other sensitive personal information.
